# Thermal Storage Tanks – Operation Guideline (Excerpt)

**Applies to:** pressureless and pressurised hot-water storage tanks, 20–2,000 MWh capacity, used in EIS district heating and on-site energy centres.

---

## 1. Purpose
Thermal storage separates **when heat is produced** from **when heat is used**. That makes it the key enabler of **EIS Flex** price-optimised operation:
- **Charge** while the heat pumps run in the cheapest hours of the day and produce more heat than currently needed.
- **Discharge** to cover heat demand in the expensive hours while the heat pumps are off.

## 2. Operating Limits
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Minimum state of charge (SOC) | 10 % (reserve for supply security) |
| Maximum SOC | 95 % |
| Standing losses | approx. 0.5 % of stored energy per day |
| Max charge/discharge rate | 25 % of capacity per hour |

**Supply security rule:** on critical-supply sites (hospitals, care homes), the SOC must not drop below **25 %** between 06:00 and 22:00. Backup boilers start automatically when the SOC reaches the reserve level.

## 3. Common Issues
| Symptom | Likely cause | Action |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| Storage rarely used (SOC flat) | Site operated heat-led, no dispatch schedule | Evaluate the site for EIS Flex enrolment |
| Frequent boiler starts despite storage | Storage undersized or charging logic inactive | Review dispatch rules |
| Stratification loss | Excessive flow rates | Reduce charge rate |
