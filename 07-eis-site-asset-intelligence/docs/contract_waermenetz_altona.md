# Heat Supply and Network Operation Agreement

**Contract No.:** EIS-C-0003
**Between:** EPOWER Infrastructure Solutions GmbH, Hamburg ("Operator")
**And:** Stadtwerke-Quartiersgesellschaft Hamburg-Altona mbH ("Customer")
**Site:** District heating network Hamburg-Altona (site ID S003)
**Contract type:** Heat supply and network operation
**Term:** 1 October 2020 – 30 September 2040 (20 years)

---

## § 1 Scope
The Operator runs the district heating network Hamburg-Altona with approx. 4,200 connected households. Generation consists of two CHP units (EcoPower EC2000, 2.0 MW_el / 2.6 MW_th each), five large heat pumps (ThermaFlux HP2000, 2.0 MW_th each) using river water as heat source, two gas boilers for peak load and backup, and a **large thermal storage tank of 400 MWh**.

## § 2 Remuneration
Annual network operation fee of **EUR 3,200,000** plus a heat price per MWh delivered under Annex 2.

## § 3 Performance Guarantees
3.1 **Availability guarantee:** heat supply availability of at least **99.5 %** (network level), measured **per calendar quarter**.

3.2 **Efficiency guarantee:** average total efficiency of the CHP units of at least **85.0 %** per calendar quarter.

3.3 **Penalty:** EUR **60,000 per percentage point** of missed supply availability per quarter. EUR **25,000 per percentage point** of missed CHP efficiency per quarter.

## § 4 EIS Flex
The network participates in the **EIS Flex** program. The Operator schedules the heat pumps based on EPEX day-ahead prices (bidding zone DE-LU): the heat pumps run preferably in the cheapest hours of each day and charge the thermal storage, which supplies heat in the remaining hours. CHP units and boilers operate heat-led. Heat demand of connected customers must be met at all times.

The flexibility margin is shared **70 % Operator / 30 % Customer**. It is credited to the customer as a heat price reduction.
