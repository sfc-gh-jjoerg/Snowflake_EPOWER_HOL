# Energy Contracting Agreement

**Contract No.:** EIS-C-0001
**Between:** EPOWER Infrastructure Solutions GmbH, Hamburg ("Contractor")
**And:** Klinikum Hamburg-Nord gGmbH, Hamburg ("Customer")
**Site:** Klinikum Hamburg-Nord energy centre (site ID S001)
**Contract type:** Energy contracting (design, finance, build, operate)
**Term:** 1 January 2019 – 31 December 2034 (16 years)

---

## § 1 Scope
The Contractor finances, installs and operates the energy centre at the Customer's site. It comprises two CHP units (EcoPower EC400, "CHP-1" and "CHP-2"), two gas boilers for backup and peak load, and a thermal storage tank of 20 MWh. The Contractor supplies heat and electricity to the hospital.

## § 2 Remuneration
The Customer pays an annual base fee of **EUR 1,850,000** plus a consumption-based heat price under Annex 2.

## § 3 Supply Security
The heat supply to the hospital has priority over any other operating objective. Backup boilers must be kept ready at all times.

## § 4 Performance Guarantees
4.1 **Availability guarantee:** the Contractor guarantees a technical availability of the CHP units of at least **97.0 %**, measured **per calendar quarter** and per CHP unit. Planned maintenance agreed with the Customer in advance doesn't count as unavailability.

4.2 **Efficiency guarantee:** the Contractor guarantees an average total efficiency of the CHP units of at least **85.0 %**, measured per calendar quarter.

4.3 **Penalty:** for each full or partial percentage point by which the guaranteed availability is missed, the Contractor pays the Customer a contractual penalty of **EUR 45,000 per CHP unit and quarter**. For each percentage point by which the efficiency guarantee is missed, the penalty is **EUR 30,000 per quarter**. The total penalty is capped at 15 % of the annual base fee per contract year.

## § 5 Flexible Operation
The site is **not** part of the EIS Flex program. The CHP units operate heat-led.

## § 6 Reporting
The Contractor provides a quarterly performance report including availability, efficiency, fault statistics and maintenance activities.
