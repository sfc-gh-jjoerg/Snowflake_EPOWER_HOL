# Energy-as-a-Service Agreement

**Contract No.:** EIS-C-0002
**Between:** EPOWER Infrastructure Solutions GmbH, Hamburg ("Provider")
**And:** Chemiepark Leuna Infrastruktur GmbH, Leuna ("Customer")
**Site:** Chemiepark Leuna process heat centre (site ID S002)
**Contract type:** Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS)
**Term:** 1 April 2021 – 31 March 2036 (15 years)

---

## § 1 Scope
The Provider operates a process heat centre consisting of one CHP unit (EcoPower EC2000), two large heat pumps (ThermaFlux HP2000, "HP-1" and "HP-2") using industrial waste heat, one gas boiler and a thermal storage tank of 60 MWh.

## § 2 Remuneration
Annual service fee of **EUR 2,400,000** plus energy prices under Annex 3.

## § 3 Performance Guarantees
3.1 **Availability guarantee:** at least **96.0 %** technical availability of the heat generation units (CHP and heat pumps), measured **per calendar quarter** as the average across all units.

3.2 **Efficiency guarantee:** the heat pumps must achieve a seasonal COP of at least **3.0**, measured per calendar quarter. For reporting, this corresponds to an efficiency KPI of 300 %.

3.3 **Penalty:** EUR **25,000 per percentage point** of missed availability per quarter. EUR **20,000 per 0.1 COP** below the guaranteed value per quarter.

## § 4 EIS Flex
The site participates in the **EIS Flex** program. The Provider may schedule the heat pumps according to day-ahead electricity prices (running in the cheapest hours and charging the thermal storage), provided the process heat demand is always met. The flexibility margin is shared **60 % Provider / 40 % Customer**.
